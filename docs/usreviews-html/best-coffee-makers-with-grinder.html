    Buying Guide: How to Choose

    Burr vs. blade grinder

    Burr grinders (Breville, Ninja Luxe) produce a more consistent grind for better flavor, while the blade-style grinders in budget drip units are fine for everyday coffee but less precise.

    Drip vs. espresso

    If you want a simple pot of coffee, a grind-and-brew drip machine like the Cuisinart is all you need. For espresso and milk drinks, step up to the Breville or Ninja Luxe.

    Capacity and programming

    Families should look for a 12-cup carafe and a programmable timer so fresh-ground coffee is ready when you wake up. Solo drinkers can save space with a 10-cup model.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Are coffee makers with built-in grinders worth it?

    Yes – grinding right before brewing noticeably improves flavor, and an all-in-one saves counter space and a second purchase versus a separate grinder.

    What’s the best grind-and-brew coffee maker?

    The Cuisinart DGB-550 is our top overall pick for its fresh grinding, 12-cup capacity, and charcoal water filter at a reasonable price.

    Can these machines make espresso too?

    Standard grind-and-brew units make drip coffee. For espresso with a built-in grinder, choose the Breville Barista Express or the 3-in-1 Ninja Luxe Café.

    The Bottom Line

    For most kitchens, the Cuisinart DGB-550 delivers fresh-ground drip coffee simply and affordably. Espresso fans should invest in the Breville Barista Express, while the Ninja Luxe Café is the do-it-all pick for anyone who wants drip, espresso, and cold brew from one machine.
