## Things to know before you buy

**Counter space is the binding constraint.** A 26-quart toaster oven is genuinely large; measure before you buy. The Breville and the Cosori are similar footprints; the dual-basket Ninjas are wider.

**Air-fry performance varies more than the marketing suggests.** Some toaster ovens run their air-fry mode with a brief preheat and a true convection fan; others just run the bake cycle and call it air-fry. The Breville and the Nuwave both deliver real air-fry performance; budget models often do not.

**The interior matters more than the exterior.** Look for a non-stick interior with a removable crumb tray. Some premium ovens use a stainless interior that is harder to clean.

**Toast mode is often an afterthought.** If you toast bread daily, the Breville and the Cuisinart are noticeably more consistent than the dual-basket Ninjas, which prioritize air-fry over toasting.

## FAQ

### Are air fryer toaster ovens worth it vs. a regular air fryer?
If you only ever air fry, no — a basket air fryer does the same job in less counter space. If you want one appliance that toasts, bakes, broils, and air fries, an air fryer toaster oven is genuinely useful.

### What size air fryer toaster oven do I need?
For one or two people, 20–26 quarts is plenty. For a family of four, 30 quarts or a dual-basket model is more useful.

### Can I put aluminum foil in an air fryer toaster oven?
Yes, but do not let it touch the heating elements. Most manuals warn against this; the reason is fire risk if the foil drifts up into the element area.

### Do air fryer toaster ovens use a lot of electricity?
Most run 1,500–1,800 W. At typical use (20–30 minutes a few times a week), the electricity cost is in the single dollars per month for most US households.

### How do I clean an air fryer toaster oven?
Most have a removable crumb tray and a non-stick interior. Wipe down after each use; run the empty oven at high heat for 10 minutes once a month to burn off residue.

### Are more expensive air fryer toaster ovens better?
Often yes, but not always. The Breville is meaningfully better than the Cuisinart; the gap between the Cuisinart and the budget Instant is smaller than the price difference suggests.
