A portable induction cooktop is one of the most useful pieces of kitchen equipment you can buy — and an underrated one. Induction heats faster than gas or electric, wastes less energy, and stays cool enough to touch a few seconds after you turn it off. For small kitchens, dorm rooms, RVs, and outdoor cooking on a patio, an induction cooktop beats nearly every alternative.

This article draws on independent published testing, manufacturer specifications, and aggregated verified-buyer feedback. Our full method is on the [Our Method](/our-method/) page; every source we relied on is listed at the bottom. We did not perform hands-on testing of any product we recommend here.

## Things to know before you buy

**Cookware compatibility is the binding constraint.** Induction only works with magnetic cookware — cast iron, most stainless steel, and induction-ready pans. If your favorite pan doesn't have an induction-ready base, you'll need an induction interface disc, which defeats most of the efficiency gain.

**Counter space and ventilation matter.** A 1,800 W induction cooktop pulls a lot of power; you need a dedicated 15-amp circuit for safe use. Don't plug one into the same outlet as another high-draw appliance.

**Wattage settings matter for low-power simmering.** A 1,800 W cooktop on its lowest setting might still be too hot for true simmering. The NuWave Flex's 600 W preset is meaningfully better for low-heat work.

**The coil size determines usable pan size.** A 6.5-inch coil is fine for a 10-inch skillet; a 5.5-inch coil is fine for an 8-inch skillet. If you regularly use a 12-inch skillet, look for a cooktop with a larger coil.

## FAQ

### Are portable induction cooktops any good?
Yes. They heat faster, run cooler, and waste less energy than any other portable cooking technology. The main downside is cookware compatibility — induction only works with magnetic pans.

### What size portable induction cooktop do I need?
For most use, a single-burner 1,500–1,800 W unit is fine. If you regularly cook for more than two people, look for a dual-burner model.

### Can I use a portable induction cooktop outdoors?
Yes, but you need a clean, dry surface and a GFCI-protected outlet. Don't use one in the rain or on a wet surface.

### Do portable induction cooktops use a lot of electricity?
At full power (1,800 W), an induction cooktop pulls about the same as a small microwave. At typical use (15–30 minutes a few times a week), the electricity cost is in the single dollars per month for most US households.

### How do I clean a portable induction cooktop?
Wipe down with a damp cloth after each use. The ceramic glass is fragile — don't use abrasive cleaners or drop heavy pans on it.

### Are more expensive induction cooktops better?
Often yes, but not always. The Breville Control Freak is meaningfully more precise than the Duxtop. The gap between the Duxtop and the NuWave Flex is smaller than the price difference suggests.
