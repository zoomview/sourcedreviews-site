## Buying Guide: What to Look For

### Cushion material

Pocket spring cushions last longer than foam. Foam compresses over time, which is why most gaming chairs need replacement after 2–3 years.

### Lumbar support

Built-in lumbar support is rare but valuable. Most gaming chairs include a lumbar pillow, which works but is less ergonomic than built-in support.

### Reclining

Standard recline is 120–155°. Some chairs recline fully flat (180°), which is good for napping.

### Armrests

Adjustable armrests are important for ergonomics. Look for chairs with 2D or 3D armrest adjustment.

### Material

PU (polyurethane) leather is the standard. Real leather is rare and expensive. Mesh is breathable but less common in gaming chairs.

## FAQ

### Are gaming chairs worth it?

For serious gamers who sit 4+ hours daily, yes. The ergonomics and posture support are real benefits.

### How long do gaming chairs last?

Cheap chairs (under $150): 1–3 years. Mid-range ($150–300): 3–5 years. Premium ($300+): 5–10 years.

### Gaming chair vs office chair — which is better?

Gaming chairs prioritize style and reclining. Office chairs prioritize ergonomics and adjustability. Serious ergonomics users may prefer office chairs.

### Should I get a chair with footrest?

If you plan to recline and game in a relaxed position, yes. Otherwise, footrests are unnecessary.
