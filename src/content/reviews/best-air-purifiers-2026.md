## Buying guide: How to choose

### Room size and coverage

Match the purifier's coverage to your room. A 200 sq ft bedroom needs a smaller unit; a 600 sq ft living room needs a larger unit. Undersized purifiers run continuously without cleaning the air. The Honeywell HPA4300W (1,718 sq ft) and VOOPNU (1,500 sq ft) cover the largest single-room areas in this roundup.

### Filtration type

True HEPA filters capture 99.97% of particles ≥0.3 microns. HEPA H13 is even stricter. Activated carbon removes odors and VOCs. For allergies, HEPA + carbon is the standard. The Honeywell HPA4300W's 4th K-Carbon layer adds a second carbon stage for VOCs.

### Smart features

App control, voice integration, and air quality sensors add convenience but increase price. If you just need filtration, a manual unit (VOOPNU, ECOSELF) works fine. Levoit covers the smart-purifier category from bedroom (Core 200S) through full-size (Core 600S).

### Noise

For bedrooms, prioritize units with sleep modes ≤25 dB. The Levoit Core 300S at 22 dB is the quietest in this roundup. Most purifiers have variable fan speeds; the lowest is usually ≤30 dB.

### Pet owners

Cylindrical intakes (Levoit, VOOPNU) work but capture less pet hair than rectangular intakes (ECOSELF). Pre-filter washability matters — Honeywell's LessMess™ design and ECOSELF's large rectangular pre-filter both reduce pet-hair buildup on the HEPA filter.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are air purifiers worth it?
For allergies, pets, wildfire smoke, or dust, yes. For clean indoor air, HEPA filtration removes 99.97% of particles ≥0.3 microns.

### What size air purifier do I need?
Match the coverage to your room. For 200 sq ft bedrooms, look for 200+ sq ft coverage. For 500 sq ft living rooms, look for 500+ sq ft coverage. The Levoit Bedroom (Core 200S) at 183 sq ft and the Levoit Core 300S at 219 sq ft are the bedroom-class picks in this roundup.

### How often do I need to replace the filter?
Most HEPA filters last 6–12 months. Activated carbon filters last 3–6 months. The Honeywell HPA4300W's LessMess™ design lets you swap the filter without dumping dust back into the room — a small but real quality-of-life upgrade.

### Are smart air purifiers worth the extra cost?
If you want auto-mode based on air quality sensors, yes — Levoit's VeSync app is the most mature in this roundup. If you just need filtration, the VOOPNU or ECOSELF manual units work fine.

### Do air purifiers help with COVID or viruses?
HEPA filters capture virus-sized particles. Air purifiers are not a substitute for ventilation, but they reduce airborne transmission.

### What is the quietest air purifier?
The Levoit Core 300S at 22 dB on sleep mode is the quietest in our roundup. The Levoit Bedroom (Core 200S) is similarly quiet at 24 dB.
