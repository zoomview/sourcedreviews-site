## Buying guide: How to choose

### Room size and coverage

Match the purifier's coverage to your room. A 200 sq ft bedroom needs a smaller unit; a 600 sq ft living room needs a larger unit. Undersized purifiers run continuously without cleaning the air.

### Filtration type

True HEPA filters capture 99.97% of particles ≥0.3 microns. HEPA H13 is even stricter. Activated carbon removes odors and VOCs. For allergies, HEPA + carbon is the standard.

### Smart features

App control, voice integration, and air quality sensors add convenience but increase price. If you just need filtration, a manual unit works fine.

### Noise

For bedrooms, prioritize units with sleep modes ≤25 dB. Most purifiers have variable fan speeds; the lowest is usually ≤30 dB.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are air purifiers worth it?
For allergies, pets, wildfire smoke, or dust, yes. For clean indoor air, HEPA filtration removes 99.97% of particles ≥0.3 microns.

### What size air purifier do I need?
Match the coverage to your room. For 200 sq ft bedrooms, look for 200+ sq ft coverage. For 500 sq ft living rooms, look for 500+ sq ft coverage.

### How often do I need to replace the filter?
Most HEPA filters last 6–12 months. Activated carbon filters last 3–6 months. Some units have washable pre-filters that last longer.

### Are smart air purifiers worth the extra cost?
If you want auto-mode based on air quality sensors, yes. If you just need filtration, a manual unit works fine.

### Do air purifiers help with COVID or viruses?
HEPA filters capture virus-sized particles. Air purifiers are not a substitute for ventilation, but they reduce airborne transmission.

### What is the quietest air purifier?
The Levoit Core 300S at 22 dB on sleep mode is the quietest in our roundup. Most purifiers have variable fan speeds; the lowest is usually ≤30 dB.
