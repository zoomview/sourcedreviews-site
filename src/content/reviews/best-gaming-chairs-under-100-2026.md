## Buying Guide: What to Look For

### Cushion material

At this price, foam is standard. Pocket spring (Dowinx) is a real upgrade.

### Reclining

Look for 120°+ recline. Flat recline (180°) is nice but not essential.

### Lumbar support

Lumbar pillows are standard at this price. Built-in lumbar support is rare.

### Armrests

Adjustable armrests are not guaranteed at this price. Fixed armrests are common.

### Warranty

Warranties at this price are typically 1 year or less.

## FAQ

### Are gaming chairs under $100 worth it?

Yes, for occasional gamers or as a starter chair. Heavy users should consider spending more.

### How long do budget gaming chairs last?

1–3 years with regular use.

### What's the best brand for budget gaming chairs?

Homall, GTRACING, and BestOffice are the most popular. Dowinx offers better cushions if you can stretch the budget.

### Should I wait for sales?

Yes — gaming chairs under $100 frequently dip to $60–80 during Prime Day, Black Friday, and other sales.

## Bottom Line

The Homall Gaming Chair is the best overall for under $100. For better cushions, the Dowinx is worth the slight price premium. For tightest budgets, BestOffice works for occasional use.
