## Buying Guide: What to Look For

### Weight capacity

Standard gaming chairs max at 250–280 lbs. Big & tall chairs support 300–400 lbs. Choose based on your weight + buffer.

### Frame construction

Steel frames are stronger than plastic. Look for chairs with steel frames and reinforced bases.

### Seat width

Wider seats are more comfortable for larger bodies. Big & tall chairs typically have 22+ inch seat widths.

### Cushion density

High-density foam lasts longer than low-density foam. Pocket spring cushions are even more durable.

### Warranty

Premium heavy-duty chairs often come with longer warranties (3–5 years).

## FAQ

### What weight capacity do I need?

Look for a chair that supports 1.5–2x your weight for safety margin.

### Are big & tall chairs wider?

Yes — typically 22+ inches wide vs 20 inches for standard chairs.

### Are heavy-duty chairs more expensive?

Generally yes. The reinforced frame and higher capacity cost more.

### Will a heavy-duty chair last longer than a standard chair?

Yes — better construction means longer lifespan.
