## Buying Guide: What to Look For

### Knife length

Most carving knives are 8–12 inches. Longer knives are better for larger roasts (whole turkey); shorter knives are more maneuverable for smaller cuts.

### Fork design

Look for a fork with long, sturdy tines and a comfortable handle. The fork holds the meat while you slice.

### Steel type

High-carbon stainless steel holds an edge better than regular stainless. German and Japanese steels are both excellent.

### Handle

Ergonomic handles reduce fatigue during long carving sessions. Pakkawood (resin-impregnated wood) is durable and beautiful. Stainless handles are dishwasher safe.

### Storage

Some sets include a storage case or box. Wooden boxes are traditional but require care. Fabric cases are simpler.

## FAQ

### How long should a carving knife be?

8–10 inches is standard. Whole turkey may benefit from 12 inches. Brisket works well with 10–12 inches.

### Are carving knives different from slicing knives?

They're similar but not identical. Carving knives are typically thicker and more rigid; slicing knives are thinner and more flexible. For most purposes, either works.

### How do I sharpen a carving knife?

Use a whetstone (1000/6000 grit) or a honing rod. Serrated carving knives need a special serrated knife sharpener.

### Can I put a carving knife in the dishwasher?

Hand-washing is recommended for high-carbon steel knives. The dishwasher can damage the edge and handle.
