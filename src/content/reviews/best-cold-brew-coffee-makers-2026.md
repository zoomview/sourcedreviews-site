## Buying Guide: What to Look For

### Filter type

Fine-mesh filters produce cleaner cups. Coarse-mesh filters let more sediment through but steep faster.

### Material

Glass is photogenic and doesn't absorb odors. Tritan plastic is durable for travel. Stainless is durable but doesn't show the concentrate.

### Capacity

Match capacity to your household. A 1.5L cold brew maker makes ~6 cups of concentrate, which dilutes to ~12 cups of ready-to-drink cold brew.

### Steep time

Most cold brew takes 12–24 hours. Some makers (like OXO) shorten this with better water distribution.

### Storage

If you want concentrate that lasts 2+ weeks, look for an airtight lid. Otherwise, transfer to a separate container.

## FAQ

### How long does cold brew last?

In an airtight container, cold brew concentrate lasts 2–3 weeks refrigerated. Diluted, ready-to-drink cold brew lasts 3–5 days.

### Is cold brew stronger than regular coffee?

Cold brew concentrate is much stronger than drip coffee (1:4 to 1:8 coffee-to-water ratio vs drip's 1:15 to 1:18). When diluted, it's similar in caffeine content to drip.

### Coarse or fine grind for cold brew?

Coarse grind is standard. Fine grind produces a cloudier cup and can clog filters.

### Does cold brew have less caffeine?

No — cold brew concentrate has more caffeine per ounce than drip. When diluted to drinking strength, caffeine content is similar.
