## Buying Guide: Posture-Friendly Features

### Lumbar support

Built-in lumbar support is best. Lumbar pillows work but require adjustment.

### Reclining

Reclining allows you to change posture during long sessions. 120°+ is essential; 155° is better.

### Armrests

Adjustable armrests keep your shoulders relaxed. 2D or 3D adjustment is preferred.

### Seat depth

Seat depth should support your thighs without pressing behind the knees. Adjustable seat depth is rare but valuable.

### Headrest

Headrests support the neck during reclined positions. Pillow headrests are standard; built-in is rare.

## FAQ

### What's the best gaming chair for back pain?

The Razer Iskur V2 X is the best for back pain because of its adaptive lumbar support.

### Are gaming chairs ergonomic?

Some are. The Razer Iskur series is genuinely ergonomic. Most gaming chairs are more aesthetic than ergonomic.

### How often should I adjust my chair?

For good posture, change position every 30–60 minutes. Reclining or standing is healthier than static sitting.

### Lumbar pillow vs built-in lumbar — which is better?

Built-in is better because it adjusts to your spine automatically. Lumbar pillows require manual adjustment.
