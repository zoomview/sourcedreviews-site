## Buying Guide: What to Look For

### Brew temperature

The most important spec is brew temperature. The Specialty Coffee Association's ideal range is 195–205°F. Many cheap brewers hit 180–185°F, which makes weak, sour coffee. Look for independent test data showing the brewer stays in range across the full cycle, not just at start.

### Carafe type

Glass carafes are cheaper and let you see the coffee, but they need a warming plate that can scorch the coffee after 30+ minutes. Thermal carafes (stainless steel, double-walled) keep coffee drinkable for 2+ hours with no warming plate.

### Capacity

Match capacity to household size. A 10-cup brewer makes ~50 oz of coffee, which is roughly two full mugs per person across three days, or one full mug across five days for one person. Going bigger than you need means stale coffee; going smaller means multiple brews.

### Programmability

If you want your coffee ready when you wake up, you need a programmable timer. Most modern brewers have this; some include auto-brew based on schedule (e.g., weekdays only).

### Certifications

SCA-certified home brewers meet specific temperature and brew-time requirements. If certification matters to you, check the SCA's database before buying.

## FAQ

### Is a more expensive coffee maker actually worth it?

Yes, but only up to a point. The Moccamaster delivers real brew-quality improvements over a $40 drip maker. But the difference between a $250 and a $400 drip maker is usually minimal — you're paying for build quality, not coffee.

### How long should a coffee maker last?

A well-built drip brewer should last 5–8 years with daily use. The Moccamaster is known for 10+ year lifespans.

### Drip vs pour-over: which is better?

Pour-over gives more control but takes more time per cup. Drip is faster and more consistent for most people. If you're serious about coffee, you might want both: a drip brewer for daily use and a pour-over setup for weekends.

### Should I buy a thermal or glass carafe?

Thermal if you regularly have leftover coffee. Glass if you drink the whole pot within 30 minutes.
