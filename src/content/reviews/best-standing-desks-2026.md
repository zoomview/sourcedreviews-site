## Buying Guide: What to Look For

### Size

Standard home office: 48×24 inches. Larger desks (55+ inches): for dual monitors or multiple people. Compact desks (32–40 inches): for small spaces.

### Adjustability

Electric adjustment is faster and easier than manual crank. Most electric desks have memory presets (saves your favorite heights).

### Stability

Frame thickness matters. Look for desks with 1.5mm+ steel frames. Larger desks need more stability.

### Weight capacity

Most standing desks support 150–300 lbs. Calculate your monitor + computer + accessories weight.

### Surface material

Solid wood is premium but expensive. MDF with laminate is the standard. Glass tops look nice but show fingerprints.

## FAQ

### How long do standing desks last?

Quality electric standing desks last 7–10 years with regular use. Motors are usually the first to fail.

### Standing desks are bad for your back?

No — but standing all day is also bad. The benefit comes from alternating between sitting and standing.

### Are standing desks worth it?

For people who work from home, yes. The flexibility to stand during calls or while reading is real.

### Manual vs electric — which is better?

Electric is faster and more convenient. Manual crank is cheaper but requires more effort each adjustment.
