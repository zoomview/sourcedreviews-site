## Buying Guide: What to Look For

### Cushion material

Pocket spring (Dowinx) is the standout at this price. Foam is standard. Mesh is rare but available.

### Lumbar support

Built-in lumbar support is rare but valuable. Most chairs include a lumbar pillow.

### Reclining

Standard is 120–155°. Full flat recline (180°) is nice for napping.

### Adjustability

2D armrests are standard; 3D is better. Height adjustment is universal.

### Warranty

Warranties at this price are typically 1–2 years.
