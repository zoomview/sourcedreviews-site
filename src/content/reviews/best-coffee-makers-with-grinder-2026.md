## Buying Guide: What to Look For

### Grinder type

Burr grinders produce more even grinds than blade grinders. Burr grinders are essential for espresso; blade grinders are acceptable for drip.

### Capacity

Match capacity to household. A 10-cup grinder brewer makes ~50 oz of coffee.

### Programmable

If you want fresh-ground coffee ready when you wake up, you need a programmable timer.

### Brew type

Drip grinder brewers are most common. Espresso machines with grinders (like the Barista Express) are a different category.

### Cleaning

Built-in grinders need occasional cleaning. Look for machines with removable grinder chambers.

## FAQ

### Are blade or burr grinders better?

Burr grinders produce more even particles, which means more even extraction. Blade grinders are cheaper but produce uneven grinds.

### How often should I clean the grinder?

Every 1–2 months, depending on use. Many grinder brewers have a self-clean cycle.

### Are coffee makers with grinders worth it?

Yes — the freshness gain is real. If you drink coffee daily, you'll notice the difference.

### Can I use pre-ground coffee in these machines?

Yes — most grinder brewers let you bypass the grinder and use pre-ground coffee.

## Bottom Line

For drip, the Cuisinart DGB-550 is the best grinder brewer for the money. For espresso, the Breville Barista Express is the gold standard. For versatility, the Ninja Luxe Café brews everything from one machine.
